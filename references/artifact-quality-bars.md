# Artifact quality bars

Use only the criteria relevant to the current artifact. Do not expand scope merely to satisfy this reference.

## Software or plugin

- Primary workflow works end to end.
- Essential errors fail clearly and safely.
- Installation or access instructions are minimal and accurate.
- Relevant tests, linting, type checks, or build checks pass.
- Security and privacy risks relevant to the workflow are addressed.

## Design or prototype

- The intended user and task are explicit.
- The primary flow is complete and understandable.
- Essential states, errors, and accessibility concerns are represented.
- A representative user can review or use it at the release location.
- The artifact communicates what is real, simulated, and unresolved.

## Documentation or playbook

- The audience and intended decision or action are explicit.
- Instructions are executable rather than aspirational.
- Examples reflect realistic use.
- Ownership, prerequisites, and handoff are clear.
- A reader can use it without live explanation.

## Research or synthesis

- The decision being supported is explicit.
- Sources and assumptions are traceable.
- Findings distinguish evidence from inference.
- The output recommends or enables a concrete next action.
- Uncertainty and unresolved risks are visible.

## Presentation or communication artifact

- The audience and desired outcome are explicit.
- The narrative has a clear beginning, tension, evidence, and resolution.
- Claims are supported.
- The artifact is usable in its intended delivery format.
- The call to action or decision is unmistakable.

## Workflow or operating system

- Trigger, inputs, steps, outputs, and owner are clear.
- The primary path has been run at least once when possible.
- Exceptions that materially affect trust are addressed.
- The workflow produces an inspectable output.
- Maintenance responsibility is defined.
