# Principles

## Pattern being interrupted

A capable creator can turn research, synthesis, systems thinking, and refinement into a delaying loop:

1. Have a compelling idea.
2. Research it thoroughly.
3. Improve the concept.
4. Build supporting systems.
5. Refine details.
6. Search for the best approach.
7. Delay the feedback only real use provides.

## Replacement loop

1. Choose one useful outcome.
2. Define a small complete artifact.
3. Build the primary path.
4. Verify it with realistic evidence.
5. Put it in another person's hands.
6. Learn and improve the next version.

## Guardrails

- Evidence creates confidence more reliably than additional preparation.
- Finished work compounds; private potential does not.
- The goal is not lower standards. Apply standards to what affects trust and use.
- Systems should support the artifact, not become a substitute for it.
- Reversible decisions should be made quickly enough to preserve momentum.
- The highest leverage comes from making other people more capable through usable tools, workflows, and experiences.
- Ask weekly: **What exists today that did not exist last Friday?**
